Privacy Policy

RF Reach and RF Reach Pro
Effective date: [DATE]  ·  Developer: [DEVELOPER NAME]

Summary: RF Reach collects no personal data and has no accounts, ads, or analytics. The app's internet access is used for exactly one purpose: downloading map and elevation tiles from third-party map servers so it can display maps and terrain. All calculations run on your device.

Information We Collect

We (the developer) collect nothing. The app has no user accounts, no analytics, no crash-reporting SDKs, no advertising, and no servers of our own. We never see who uses the app or how it is used.

Location Data

If you grant the location permission, your device location is used only on your device to center the map and set your station position. Your location is never transmitted to us or to any third party as location data. You can decline the permission and enter coordinates manually; the app remains fully functional.

Internet Use: Map and Elevation Tiles

To display maps and terrain, the app requests map image tiles and elevation data tiles from third-party servers, in the same way a web browser requests images from a website:

Basemap tiles are served by CARTO (using OpenStreetMap data), and elevation tiles are served from the AWS Open Data Terrain Tiles dataset hosted on Amazon Web Services.

When the app requests a tile, those servers necessarily receive your device's IP address and the coordinates of the map area being requested, as part of the normal operation of any internet request. We do not add any identifying information to these requests, and no account, name, or personal detail is sent. The handling of these requests is governed by the providers' own policies: CARTO Privacy Policy, AWS Privacy Notice, and the OpenStreetMap Foundation Privacy Policy (for map data attribution).

Offline Use

RF Reach Pro allows you to download map and elevation tiles for an area in advance. Once downloaded, the app can operate fully offline with no network activity. Downloaded tiles, station profiles, and settings are stored only in the app's private storage on your device and are removed when you uninstall the app.

Third-Party Services

Other than the tile servers described above, the app contains no third-party services, SDKs, or code that communicates over a network. There are no advertising networks and no analytics.

Children's Privacy

The app collects no personal data from any user, including children.

Purchases

If you purchased RF Reach Pro through Google Play, that transaction is handled entirely by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details. Google's handling of purchase data is described in Google's Privacy Policy.

Changes to This Policy

If a future version of the app changes how it handles data, this policy will be updated and the effective date above revised.
